2. What Nomozi does

Nomozi helps you organize a weekly dinner plan, view recipes, create a grocery list and estimate shopping costs. The available features use your selected budget, household size, diet and excluded foods.

Plans are generated from the app's available recipe catalog. The catalog and your selected restrictions limit the possible results. An estimate may exceed your requested budget; the app displays an over-budget notice where applicable. Nomozi does not purchase groceries, reserve stock or obtain a binding retailer quotation for you.

Changing your preferences does not necessarily change a plan you have already accepted. Use the app's review and rebuild controls to apply changes to a new plan.

Nomozi is a planning tool, not medical advice, a personalized clinical nutrition service or an allergen-certification system. A diet label or excluded-food selection is based on the app's catalog information. It does not inspect a packaged product's label or establish the absence of allergens or cross-contact in manufacturing or cooking.

Check ingredient and allergen labels, substitutions, preparation instructions and the suitability of the food for the people who will eat it.
