No Nomozi account or active subscription is required to contact support.

Contact support

In the app, open Settings → Support & Privacy. You can also open support from the subscription paywall, unfinished onboarding and blocking recovery screens. Use Copy Support ID if an ID is available.

Your Support ID is the current Adapty technical profile identifier. It helps us find the relevant technical profile; it does not grant app access or prove authority to delete data. If the ID is unavailable, you can still email us.

For an app issue, describe what happened and what you expected. Do not send Apple passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details or unnecessary medical or dietary information.

Manage or cancel an Apple subscription

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone and tap your name.
  2. Open Subscriptions and choose Nomozi.
  3. Follow Apple’s options to manage or cancel your subscription.

Review the price, billing period and any offer in Apple’s purchase interface before buying. Follow Apple’s cancellation timing for your subscription or offer. Read Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Deleting the app or requesting data deletion does not cancel your Apple subscription. Subscription billing is separate from grocery estimates shown in Nomozi.

Restore purchases and request a refund

Use Restore purchases in Nomozi’s Settings or on its subscription paywall, using the App Store account associated with the purchase. An internet connection may be required. Restore checks eligible purchases; it does not buy a new subscription or restore your local meal plan from a Nomozi server.

For a purchase billed by Apple, submit refund requests through Apple’s refund service. Eligibility and the outcome depend on Apple’s process and applicable law. You can also email us about an app problem.

Request deletion of server-held data

  1. If available, copy your Support ID from Support & Privacy. Save it before deleting the app, because a later installation may have a different ID.
  2. Tap Request data deletion. The app opens a draft addressed to arifsadykov7@gmail.com, with a request subject and the available Support ID. You send the message yourself.
  3. If a mail app is unavailable, use Copy email and Copy Support ID, then send the request from another email service. An unavailable ID does not prevent you from contacting us.

You can also start a deletion-request email here and add your Support ID if available.

This is a request, not immediate deletion. We need to locate the relevant data and separately verify your authority before acting. A Support ID alone is not sufficient proof. If reliable verification is not possible, we will explain the limitation. We do not ask for Apple passwords, authentication codes or payment-card details.

Adapty provides profile-deletion tools, but profile deletion does not establish immediate physical removal of every transaction record, log or backup. No fixed deletion timetable for those copies has been confirmed. A request to Nomozi does not erase Apple’s own records or cancel a subscription.

Remove local planning data

On iPhone, open Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Nomozi → Delete App. Offload App keeps documents and data. Reset onboarding inside Nomozi is not a full data-deletion command. Manage device and iCloud backups separately. See Apple’s storage guidance.

Deleting the app does not itself request deletion of your Adapty profile. Read our Privacy Policy for local data, service providers, support email and retention, and our Terms of Use for the app and subscriptions.
