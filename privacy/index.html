1. About this policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Nomozi iOS application, initially offered in the United States and Canada. “We”, “us” and “our” refer to Arif Sadykov, the individual operator of Nomozi.

Nomozi does not require a separate Nomozi account, email address or password. Nevertheless, subscription services use technical identifiers to recognize an installation and associate it with purchases. The absence of a Nomozi login does not mean that all information is anonymous or stays on your device.

2. Meal-planning information stored on your device

Nomozi stores the following information locally using iOS application storage:

  • Your weekly budget, household size, diet selection and excluded foods.
  • Your weekly meal plan, including recipe identifiers and the preferences applied when that plan was accepted.
  • Grocery-list information, including product identifiers, quantities, package information and “Purchased” or “Already have” selections.
  • Onboarding progress and completion status.
  • Subscription-related service state used by the application. A saved local access value is not, by itself, proof of an active subscription.

The current application does not send your budget, diet selection, excluded foods, selected recipes or grocery-list selections to Adapty. It does not provide its own cloud account or CloudKit synchronization for this information.

Your device or computer backups may contain application data. For example, Apple may include local application data in iCloud Backup when you have enabled that service. Backups are managed through your Apple settings and are separate from Nomozi's own data storage.

3. Subscription and technical information handled through Adapty

Nomozi uses Adapty to provide subscription services and subscription/paywall analytics. The SDK is activated when the application starts, so technical information can be processed before you buy a subscription.

The current integration processes the following categories:

Technical identifiers

Adapty profile ID, installation ID, session ID and Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV)

Purpose: Recognize an installation or profile and associate subscription activity with it

Device and application information

Device model, operating-system version, app version/build, SDK version, language/locale, time zone, App Store country, available user-agent information and App Tracking Transparency status

Purpose: Operate the service, deliver compatible configurations and analyze subscription activity

Usage and paywall events

App openings, paywall-variant assignment and paywall displays, including identifiers and timestamps

Purpose: Understand subscription and paywall performance

Purchase and subscription information

Product ID, transaction/original transaction ID, price, currency, region, offer and StoreKit environment; resulting subscription status

Purpose: Validate purchases, restore purchases and determine paid access

Adapty also keeps local identifiers, an installation timestamp and launch counter, cached profiles and subscription information, event queues, purchase-synchronization information and configuration caches.

The application does not send your name, email address or telephone number to Adapty, and does not attach a Nomozi account ID or custom user attributes. Technical profile data can still be personal information.

Identifier settings and Apple Ads

The current app disables Adapty’s IDFA collection and does not display advertising or request App Tracking Transparency permission. Its SDK configuration also disables optional IP-address collection. This setting does not establish that no IP addresses are processed: network connections expose IP addresses, and provider-side settings and security logs may involve IP processing. We do not represent IP-address collection as completely disabled across Adapty’s service.

On supported devices, the Adapty SDK attempts to obtain an Apple Ads attribution token through Apple’s AdServices framework and, if successful, sends it to Adapty associated with the technical profile identifier. This may be used to associate an app download with an Apple advertising campaign. Apple Ads attribution is separate from IDFA collection; disabling IDFA does not by itself disable this token flow.

4. Apple purchases and payments

Subscriptions are purchased and restored using Apple's StoreKit services. Apple handles Apple Account and payment information under its own terms and privacy practices. Nomozi does not provide fields for your payment-card details or Apple Account password and does not receive those credentials through its purchase interface.

Nomozi and Adapty receive the purchase and subscription information needed to verify access. Apple maintains its own account and transaction records; a request to Nomozi does not delete Apple's records or cancel a subscription.

See Apple's App Store privacy information.

5. Support communications

If you contact us at arifsadykov7@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your Support ID and a description of your request. We use this information to answer you, investigate problems and handle privacy requests.

Our support mailbox uses Gmail. Google processes email content, addresses, attachments and associated technical information to provide its email service under its Privacy Policy. Send only information needed for your request.

Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, authentication codes or sensitive dietary or medical information that is not necessary for your request. A support request may require limited technical information to distinguish your installation or transaction from someone else's.

6. Who handles the information

Adapty processes the subscription, identifier and event information described above as a service provider on our behalf. Its published End-Users Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement describe its processing framework.

Apple separately processes information for its App Store, payment and backup services. Google provides our Gmail support mailbox, and GitHub hosts this website. Their respective services and processing are also described in their own privacy disclosures.

We may need to disclose relevant information when required by applicable law, or to establish, exercise or defend legal rights. Any future change of operator would require an updated privacy notice and any additional steps required by applicable law; the current operator is Arif Sadykov.

7. Retention

Local planning information. The current app does not automatically remove your meal plan and preferences after a fixed number of days. They remain in local application storage until changed or removed. “Reset onboarding” is not a full deletion feature: it retains your preferences, plan and subscription-related state.

Adapty information. Deleting Nomozi does not itself request deletion of your Adapty profile. Adapty's published documents tie retention to the processing purpose, its customer contract and applicable legal requirements. We do not promise a fixed deletion deadline for every event, transaction record, log or backup that has not been confirmed for this service.

Support correspondence. Support messages are stored in our Gmail mailbox and used to respond to and follow up on requests. There is no fixed automatic deletion period for support correspondence. You can ask us to delete correspondence, subject to verification and any applicable retention requirements.

Information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or to address an unresolved legal claim. We do not state a specific retention period for such records without a confirmed basis.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

Change planning information. You can edit your preferences and grocery selections using the controls provided in Nomozi.

Remove local app data. On iPhone, use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Nomozi → Delete App. Apple's “Offload App” option retains documents and data. Backups must be managed separately. See Apple's storage guidance.

Cancel a subscription. Use Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Nomozi. Deleting or offloading the app, resetting onboarding, or requesting profile deletion does not cancel billing. See Apple's cancellation guidance.

Request access, correction or deletion. Contact arifsadykov7@gmail.com. Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a copy of, restrict or object to processing of your information, and to complain to an appropriate authority. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

In Nomozi, open Settings → Support & Privacy → Copy Support ID. Support is also available from the subscription paywall, unfinished onboarding and blocking recovery screens, without a purchase. The Support ID is the current Adapty profile identifier and helps locate technical data. If it is unavailable, you can still contact us. If possible, save it before deleting the app; a later installation may have a different identifier.

The Support ID is a lookup identifier, not proof that you are authorized to delete the profile. Because Nomozi has no login, an email address alone does not establish ownership either. We need to verify your authority separately and may request the minimum relevant information for that purpose. Do not send Apple passwords, authentication codes or payment-card details. If we cannot reliably identify the data or verify your authority, we will explain the limitation rather than act on someone else’s profile.

Request data deletion in Support & Privacy opens an email draft that you send yourself. It includes the request subject and the available Support ID. If a mail app is unavailable, copy the email address and Support ID and use another email service. This is a request, not immediate deletion.

Verified, applicable requests can be handled using Adapty’s profile-deletion tools. Adapty has confirmed that a successful profile deletion can leave an empty profile response available through its API. Profile deletion does not establish immediate physical erasure of every transaction record, log or backup, and no fixed deletion timetable for those copies has been confirmed. It does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple’s own records.

9. Security and processing locations

The application uses system HTTPS facilities for communication with Adapty. Local information is handled through iOS application storage and device protections. No application or internet service can guarantee absolute security.

We operate from Serbia. Adapty, Apple, Google and GitHub infrastructure can involve processing outside your country. This policy does not promise that all information remains in one country.

10. Children and eligibility

Nomozi provides general-audience meal-planning tools and is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask users to create a Nomozi account or provide their date of birth. Apple's App Store age rating describes the suitability of app content; it is not a statement that a child may independently enter into a paid subscription. Parents and guardians should supervise use and purchases by minors as required by their local laws and Apple's purchase controls. We do not intentionally request children's names or direct contact details within the app. If you have a concern about a child's information, contact us at arifsadykov7@gmail.com.

See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.
